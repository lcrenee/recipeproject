Recipe on Wasting Time

A commuter's edition on how to spend your time wisely during a long but not long enough gap to make a trip back home or wherever you are staying!

Ingredients:

No Sugar
No Flour
No Oil
No Milk
Some worthy time
$10 Bill

Steps:

1. Get off class and make your way to the nearest elevator or flight of stairs.
2. Make your way to the streets of the city by selecting to go to the ground floor of your elevator, or walking to the entrance of your school building through stairs exit.
3. Walk to a plain wall, and open maps on your phone.
5. Search for the boba shop of your choice and locate to destination. Make sure that the shop is in walkable distance.
6. Walk to the boba shop.
7. After arriving, walk into the store and stand at a place where you are able to decide what to drink.
8. Order and pay for your drinks. Don't forget to send a picture to your friends to show off the yummy drink in your hand.
9. Now, for a new destination...with seats avaliable, preferably a comfortable one. Locate to the school library or cafeteria.
10. Arrive at the spot, and find a seating.
11. Now that you are in a comfortable chair, open the timers app.
12. Set a time just 10 minutes before your class starts, so we are there early and on time for the next class.
13. Close timer and open tiktok.
14. Doomscroll until timers off.
15. Get your last laugh in before your trip back to class.
